# DepEd MATATAG Araling Panlipunan CG, Grades 4 and 7. Source: https://deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/MATATAG-Araling-Panlipunan-CG-Grade-4-and-7.pdf
# Competencies are shortened English glosses of the Filipino text; UNVERIFIED by a person.
## 4|1
Maps and globe|Locate the Philippines using absolute and relative location
National territory|Explain the bases of the extent and boundaries of Philippine territory
Concept of a nation|Discuss the concept of a nation
Geographic identity|Describe the geographic identity of the Philippines (physical and human geography)
Location and geography|Analyze how the Philippines' location relates to its geography
Geography and development|Analyze how geographic features relate to national development
## 4|2
Natural resources|Identify the natural resources found in the country
Economic activities|Explain the economic benefits of natural resources
Economic challenges|Discuss challenges and responses in the country's economic activities
Sustainable development|Identify challenges to achieving sustainable development
Sustainable development|Value activities that promote sustainable use of resources
## 4|3
Elements of a nation|Discuss the importance of the elements of a nation (people, territory, government, sovereignty)
National government|Analyze the structure of the Philippine government (unitary presidential, separation of powers)
Government services|Analyze the role of government and its health, education, peace, economic and infrastructure programs
Government programs|Value the various government programs
## 4|4
National symbols|Explain the symbols of Filipino identity
Citizenship|Identify the concept and principles of citizenship
Rights and duties|Discuss the rights and duties of a Filipino citizen
Civic efficacy|Discuss the concept of civic efficacy
Civic efficacy|Value the role of citizens in achieving civic efficacy
## 7|1
Geography and ancient history|Explain the role of physical features in early history and culture of the Philippines and Southeast Asia
Geography and ancient history|Analyze human geography of Southeast Asia: ethnolinguistic groups, beliefs, social structures
Geography and ancient history|Relate ancient social structure to kinship, family, and gender roles
Geography and ancient history|Analyze Austronesian culture and maritime empires in shaping Philippine and SE Asian culture
Geography and ancient history|Relate ancient Philippine civilization to SE Asian, Chinese, and Indian civilizations
## 7|2
Colonialism and imperialism|Explain the concepts of colonialism and imperialism
Colonialism and imperialism|Compare the first and second phases of Western imperialism
Colonialism and imperialism|Analyze colonial methods and policies in the Philippines, Indonesia, and Malaysia
Colonialism and imperialism|Analyze responses to colonial rule (revolt, claim, adaptation) in island Southeast Asia
Colonialism and imperialism|Analyze colonial methods and responses in Cambodia, Myanmar, and Vietnam
Colonialism and imperialism|Explain Japanese imperialism in the 20th century
## 7|3
Nationalism, independence and nationhood|Explain nationalism, independence, and nationhood
Nationalism, independence and nationhood|Explain how selected Southeast Asian countries attained independence
Nationalism, independence and nationhood|Analyze challenges to Philippine nationhood after World War II
Nationalism, independence and nationhood|Analyze nationhood challenges in Southeast Asia after the Bandung Conference
## 7|4
ASEAN|Discuss ASEAN's aims, history, structure, and key achievements
ASEAN|Relate the Philippines' role as an active ASEAN member
ASEAN|Analyze ASEAN's challenges and responses in achieving sustainable development
ASEAN|Analyze ASEAN's role in human rights issues in the Philippines and Southeast Asia
